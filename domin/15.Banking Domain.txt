Banking Domain:
---------------
Core Banking, Corporate Banking, Personal Banking

Modules:
--------
1.User Management Module:
-------------------------
Allows users to create accounts, log in, and manage their profiles.
Stores user information, account settings, and preferences.
Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): multiple forms of verification.

2.Account Management Module:
----------------------------
Provides a summary of all user accounts (e.g., savings, fixed, loans).
Displays detailed records of past transactions.
Generates and provides access to periodic account statements.

3.Funds Transfer Module:
------------------------
Enables transfers between the user's own accounts.
Facilitates transfers to other bank accounts, both within the same bank and to other banks.
Allows users to set up recurring or future-dated transfers.

4.Bill Payment Module:
----------------------
Manages a list of payees for bill payments.
Allows users to schedule one-time or recurring bill payments.
Displays a history of past bill payments.

5.Card Management Module:
-------------------------
Allows users to activate or deactivate their debit/credit cards.
Enables users to set spending limits and manage card settings.

6.Loan Management Module:
-------------------------
Facilitates the process of applying for personal, auto, or home loans.
Displays the repayment schedule and allows users to make payments.
Provides updates on the status of loan applications and repayments.

7.Customer Support Module:
--------------------------
Offers FAQs, guides, and support resources.
Provides real-time chat support and secure messaging with customer service representatives.
Allows users to submit and track support tickets.

8.Alerts and Notifications Module:
----------------------------------
Sends notifications for important account activities (e.g., low balance, large transactions).
Notifies users of suspicious activities and potential security threats.
Sends reminders for upcoming payments, transfers, and other scheduled activities.
