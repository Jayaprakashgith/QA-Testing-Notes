Travel Domain:
--------------
Different modes of travels like road, water, rail and air.

Modules:
--------
1.User Management Module:
-------------------------
Allows users to create accounts, log in, and manage their profiles.
Stores user information, booking history, and preferences.
Manages different access levels and permissions for users (e.g., guest, frequent flyer, travel agent).

2.Search and Filtering Module:
------------------------------
Enables users to search for travel options using various criteria (e.g., destination, dates, budget).
Provides options to filter results by attributes (e.g., price, rating, travel duration) and sort them accordingly.

3.Booking Module:
-----------------
Allows users to search, compare, and book tickets.

4.Payment and Checkout Module:
------------------------------
Supports multiple payment methods (e.g., credit card, PayPal, bank transfer).
Sends confirmation emails and receipts to users after a successful booking.

5.Reviews and Ratings Module:
-----------------------------
Allows customers to leave reviews and ratings for flights, hotels, car rentals, and other services.
Moderates and manages customer feedback.

6.Customer Support Module:
--------------------------
Provides FAQs, guides, and support resources.
Offers live chat support and a system for submitting and tracking support tickets.

7.Notifications Module:
-----------------------
Sends notifications about booking confirmations, cancellations, and changes.
Provides reminders for upcoming trips, check-in times, and other important information.

8.Loyalty and Rewards Module:
-----------------------------
Rewards frequent travelers with points, discounts, or special offers.
Incentivizes users to refer friends and family to the platform.
