Ecommerce Domain:
------------------
Online shopping Platform

Modules:
--------
1.User Management Module:
--------------------------
Allows users to create accounts, log in, and manage their profiles.
Stores user information, order history, and preferences.

2.Product Management Module:
----------------------------
Organizes products into categories and subcategories for easy navigation.
Manages product listings, descriptions, images, and prices.

3.Search and Filtering Module:
------------------------------
Allows users to search for products using keywords.
Provides options to filter products by various attributes (e.g., price, brand, ratings) and sort them accordingly.

4.Shopping Cart Module:
-----------------------
Enables users to add, update, or remove products from their shopping cart.
Saves the state of the cart even if the user logs out or the session expires.

5.Checkout and Payment Module:
------------------------------
Guides users through the steps of purchasing products, including shipping and billing information.
Supports multiple payment methods (e.g., credit card, PayPal, bank transfer).
Sends confirmation emails and receipts to users after a successful purchase.

6.Order Management Module:
--------------------------
Allows users to track the status of their orders from processing to delivery.
Provides a record of past purchases for users to review.

7.Shipping Module:
------------------
Offers various shipping methods and calculates shipping costs.

8.Reviews and Ratings Module:
-----------------------------
Allows customers to leave reviews and ratings for products.
Moderates and manages customer feedback.

9.Customer Support Module:
--------------------------
Provides FAQs, guides, and support resources.
Offers live chat support and a system for submitting and tracking support tickets.

10.Marketing and Promotions Module:
-----------------------------------
Manages promotional offers, discounts, and coupon codes.
Sends newsletters and promotional emails to customers.
Rewards loyal customers with points, discounts, or special offers.
